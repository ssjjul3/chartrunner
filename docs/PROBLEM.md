# Problem Validation

## The Hospital Monitor Problem

Open a professional trading terminal for the first time. The screen assumes you already understand candles, position sizing, stops, brackets, order types, indicators, and risk.

Most new traders do not need another dashboard. They need reps.

ChartRunner turns those reps into a game loop: run on the chart, use trading primitives as abilities, get immediate feedback, and learn the shape of a trade before any production execution is involved.

## What People Do Instead

- Watch videos, which are passive.
- Read guides, which are static.
- Try paper trading, which often feels like the same intimidating terminal with fake money.
- Copy other traders, which skips the learning loop entirely.

None of those surfaces make the act of trading feel playable.

## ChartRunner's Bet

The next wave of traders will not learn from PDFs alone. They will learn from interactive practice where the input, feedback, mistake, and retry happen in one place.

The public product thesis is simple:

- The playable demo teaches the muscle memory.
- `ChartRunnerSDK` keeps every trade-like action on one intent path.
- Wallet/devnet proof shows the approval boundary.
- Premium execution, data, bots, and commercial rails stay gated until deliberately released.

## Who Feels This Most

| Segment | Pain | Why ChartRunner Fits |
|---|---|---|
| New crypto users | Wallets work, trading risk does not | The game teaches risk as a mechanic |
| Streaming-era new traders | Watched hours of content, still afraid to act | Every run is hands-on practice |
| Chart-first users | Charts are visible, training is not | The SDK boundary can sit on chart hosts later |

## Validation We Can Share Publicly

- Session length in the playable prototype.
- Repeat runs and campaign completion.
- Qualitative feedback about whether the mechanic makes brackets, stops, and risk clearer.
- Devnet proof that wallet-approved actions remain explicit.

Private traction targets, outreach plans, partner conversations, and commercial packaging live outside the public repo.
