> one plan. everything.

Free to play. Free to trade. Pro when you want the edge.

The game stays free forever — no pay-to-win. Trading, copytrade, rooms & maps are all free, monetized by a small per-trade fee like every Solana terminal. Runner Pro unlocks the full data layer (Intel, whale feed, backtesting, API + RPC throughput), higher capacities — and cuts your per-trade fee ~30% (0.5% → 0.35%), so for active traders it pays for itself (deeper cuts via $RUN staking). Pay by card via Stripe, or on-chain in SOL / USDC. $RUN lowers fees and prices further (coming).

Annual = 2 months free

How fulfillment works: paying here starts checkout only. Card checkout mints a real Stripe Checkout Session when you're signed in, so your Pro grant is automatic; anonymous card links are being reconfigured for the new prices, so sign in on /play for card checkout in the meantime. On-chain pay builds a Solana Pay request to the ChartRunner treasury — confirming the payment and granting Pro is verified server-side and lands with live trading (Phase 2). The game itself is always free and never pay-to-win; the Pro fee-discount applies to real on-chain trading fees, not game outcomes.
