ChartRunnerOS

> one plan. everything.

Free to play. Free to trade. Pro when you want the edge.

The game stays free forever — no pay-to-win. Trading, copytrade, rooms & maps are all free, monetized by a small per-trade fee like every Solana terminal. Runner Pro unlocks the full data layer (Intel, whale feed, backtesting, API + RPC throughput), higher capacities — and will cut your per-trade fee ~30% (0.5% → 0.35%) once Pro pricing goes live on the trade path (planned), so for active traders it will pay for itself (deeper cuts via $RUN staking). Pay by card via Stripe, or on-chain in SOL / USDC. $RUN lowers fees and prices further (coming).

Annual = 2 months free

How fulfillment works: Card runs through Stripe and works with or without an account: signed in, the worker mints a real Checkout Session and your Pro grant is automatic; signed out, the payment link carries the same price and the purchase is claimable in-game with the same e-mail. On-chain pay is issued per account and therefore needs you signed in: the ChartRunner server hands out the receiving address, the exact amount and a payment reference — this page builds none of them — then watches that reference, and Pro switches on as soon as the server confirms the payment. Until it does, nothing is claimed: the page shows what it is waiting for and keeps the reference in view. The game itself is always free and never pay-to-win; the Pro fee-discount applies to real on-chain trading fees, not game outcomes.

Coach note: ChartRunner is entertainment and education — but the trading is not a simulation: orders you arm and sign in the game execute on Solana mainnet, from your own wallet, with your own funds.
