Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-05-29. Covers chartrunner.xyz, the in-browser game at /play, and the Runner Wallet Chrome extension.

What we collect

Almost nothing. ChartRunner is built so that the data we'd otherwise need to ask you for either doesn't exist, lives on a public blockchain, or stays on your own device.

Anonymous analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to count page views and basic traffic patterns. Plausible does not set cookies, does not collect personal data, and does not track you across sites. The data we see is aggregated: page paths, country-level location, browser and OS family, and approximate session counts.

Wallet addresses

When you connect a Solana wallet to the game or to Runner Wallet, your wallet's public address becomes visible to ChartRunner (and to anyone, because it's on the public Solana ledger). We do not collect, store, or transmit your private keys, seed phrases, or any other secret material. Wallet signatures stay between your wallet software and the Solana network.

On-chain interactions

When you save a map, list a license, buy a license, or record a run, those interactions are written to the Solana blockchain at your direction. They are public by design. We do not add additional tracking metadata.

Local browser storage

The game uses your browser's localStorage to remember in-game settings, your last run, and (if you used the "Continue as Guest" path) a locally generated guest ID. None of this data leaves your device.

What we don't collect

  • Names, emails, phone numbers, mailing addresses, payment cards, or any contact information.
  • Cookies for tracking or advertising. (Plausible doesn't use cookies; we don't run ad networks.)
  • Your private keys, seed phrases, mnemonics, or wallet passphrases. We never see them; they never leave your wallet software.
  • Cross-site browsing history.
  • Camera, microphone, or location beyond country-level analytics.

Runner Wallet (Chrome extension)

Runner Wallet is a self-custody Solana wallet that runs entirely inside your browser.

Key handling

Your Solana keypair is generated or imported on your device and stored encrypted in chrome.storage.local behind a passphrase you choose. We have no way to recover that passphrase if you lose it. Keys never leave your browser.

Network requests

To show balances and submit transactions, the extension talks directly to public Solana RPC endpoints (mainnet-beta or devnet, depending on your selection). It also reads your wallet-held NFTs through a public NFT API provider. These are the only outbound requests the extension makes.

Game integration

On chartrunner.xyz/play, Runner Wallet exposes a wallet adapter so the game can request signatures from you. The extension does not auto-sign anything — every transaction shows a confirmation popup that you can review and reject. The game never sees your keys.

LLM panel

The optional in-extension LLM panel routes your messages to whichever model you configure — by default a locally hosted Ollama instance on your own machine. If you configure a remote model, your messages go to that provider directly; ChartRunner does not see or store them.

Payments

If you use the in-extension payment helpers (for example, paying for an oracle-approved backtest), the SOL or token amount is transferred from your wallet to the recipient on the public Solana ledger. We do not custody funds on your behalf; we do not see card or bank information.

Third parties

The only third parties we rely on are:

  • Plausible Analytics — anonymous traffic counting on the website.
  • Solana RPC providers — to read balances and submit transactions to the blockchain you chose.
  • NFT API providers — to display your wallet-held NFTs in the avatar picker.
  • Your LLM provider (only if you configure one in Runner Wallet's LLM panel) — your messages go to that provider, not to us.

We do not sell, rent, or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

ChartRunner involves real on-chain assets when used outside guest mode and is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date at the top of this page when this policy changes. Material changes will be flagged in our changelog and on the landing page.
