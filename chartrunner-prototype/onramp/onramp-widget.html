Advanced — already hold crypto?

No onramp account needed. If you already own SOL or USDC on a centralized exchange, just withdraw to your wallet:

  1. Buy SOL or USDC on Coinbase or Kraken (KYC happens at the exchange, not here).
  2. Open Withdraw → pick the Solana network → paste your wallet address above → send.
  3. Funds arrive on-chain in seconds. Done — no ChartRunner account, no extra KYC.

Already on-chain with the wrong token? Swap SOL ⇄ USDC directly on Jupiter (jup.ag) or Orca (orca.so) — connect wallet, swap, no signup. On-chain-first, always.
