What is ChartRunner?

A platformer where the level is the market.

The ground you run on is a real price chart. Time it right and you fly; mistime it and the bears catch you. It's pick-up-and-play in 10 seconds — and quietly teaches you how markets move. No jargon required.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes — press Play and you're in. No install, no sign-up, no ads, no pay-to-win.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. You can play everything as a guest. Connecting a Solana wallet is optional and only used to save your profile and (if you choose) record a best run on-chain.

Is this real trading? Will I lose money?

No. Every trade in the game is simulated. ChartRunner is a game — it doesn't touch real funds and isn't a place to trade. See the disclaimer.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — it runs in the mobile browser with on-screen controls. Press Play.

Will I actually learn anything?

That's the idea: the gameplay mirrors how price, volatility and risk really behave, so the instincts you build are real — minus the real-money risk.
