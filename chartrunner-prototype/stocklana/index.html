How it works

1 · Pick a stock

Every tile is a Solana token. Tap it to start.

2 · Run the chart

ChartRunner loads the token's real candles and drops you into a live run. No sign-up, you play as a guest.

3 · Trade for real, if you want

Connect a wallet in the game and the abilities become real Jupiter orders, signed by you. Without a wallet everything is simulated.

Not financial advice. Play is simulated unless you connect a wallet and sign a trade yourself. Tokenized and pre-IPO stocks carry issuer and liquidity risk and may not be available where you live (xStocks are not offered to US persons).
