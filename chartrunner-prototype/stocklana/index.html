Why this belongs on Solana

24/7 is the product

A brokerage app goes dark at 4pm and on weekends. Tokenized stocks don't. The arcade and the gap radar only make sense on a chain that never closes.

One wallet, every issuer

xStocks, PreStocks and Tessera are SPL tokens. The same wallet holds all of them, and Jupiter routes between them. That's why a cross-issuer spread board is even possible.

Self-custodial by design

This page signs nothing. In the full ChartRunner, every live trade is the player's own click and wallet signature, routed through Jupiter.

Learn by playing

Calling candles on real stock tokens teaches trend, gap and volatility faster than a watchlist does, and the score is only ever simulated.

Not financial advice. Scores are simulated. This page reads public market data and never requests a wallet signature. Tokenized stocks and pre-IPO tokens carry issuer, liquidity and structural risk and may not be available in your jurisdiction (xStocks, for example, are not offered to US persons). Pre-IPO tokens track SPV exposure, not direct shares.
